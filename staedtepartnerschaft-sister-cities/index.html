As I collected photos of people and places during my year living in Germany as a Congress-Bundestag Young Professional Fellow, I imagined a project and pitched it to the City of Celle, the German sister city of my hometown, Tulsa. To my luck they provided me with a desk in City Hall, a salary, and colleagues who graciously welcomed me into their community. I began creating Städtepartnerschaft, sister cities - a photographic love letter to the people of Germany and the reflections of home I saw there. During my time in Germany, I arranged and photographed over 40 shoots.

Liebe Grüße, McKinleigh Lair
